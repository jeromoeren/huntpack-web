Share your location

You got this link by text message from someone in a wildlife team using the HuntPack app. They are following up on a wildlife incident (for example a collision with an animal) and need to know exactly where it happened.

Stand at the place, or as close as you can, and tap the button. Your phone will ask if it may share your location – choose Allow.

Your location is sent once, only when you tap the button. Nothing else is shared.

If you can't share your location, call back the person who sent you the link and describe the place.
