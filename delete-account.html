HuntPack – Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open HuntPack.
  2. Open the menu (☰) and go to Account → Delete account.
    Without a team, tap Delete account on the Hunting teams screen.
  3. Confirm twice. The account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Send an email to support@huntpack.app from the email address you use in HuntPack, with the subject Delete my HuntPack account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Your positions, tracks and battery level
  • Messages to go to a spot, invitations and your hunt day reports
  • Teams where you are the only member

Marks and maps you added stay on the team map without your name, because the rest of the team uses them.
